# Thinking Roadmap — Three Big Questions

**Priority: VERY IMPORTANT.** Three foundational questions Thức committed to on
2026-07-08. Worked **strictly in sequence** — one at a time, no parallel
processing ("dễ bị loãng"). Each question closes with concept pages in the wiki
and, where applicable, mechanism changes to this brain's schema.

Origin note: these emerged when Thức judged he lacked the foundations to
critique the Hermes/Shepherd/Codez architecture claims — and chose to step back
to basics instead of nodding along. That move is itself metacognition in
action.

## Question 1 — Metacognition (thinking about your thinking)

**Thức's starting position:** a correct discipline to follow; helps humans,
this brain, and agents recognize problems, get to the root, and self-develop.

**Agreed plan:**
1. Ground the concept: what metacognition actually is (monitoring vs. control),
   how it differs from plain [[wiki/reflection-and-depth]], and its failure
   mode — rumination without a stopping rule.
2. Make it operational: what a *structured* metacognitive move looks like for
   (a) Thức personally, (b) this brain's operations, (c) agents in a pipeline.
3. Deliverables: `wiki/metacognition.md` concept page; updates to
   [[wiki/how-i-want-to-think]] if the practice card gains a new move.

## Question 2 — Fact vs. opinion, with separate stores

**Thức's starting position:** without the right frame we may adopt architecture
*opinions* (Hermes, Shepherd, …) as if they were facts, freezing them into the
brain/agents and shrinking the growth space. Build a distinguishing mechanism
and separate stores (kho riêng) for each.

**Agreed plan:**
1. Ground the concepts: the spectrum fact → inference → hypothesis → opinion
   (not a binary); how his personal trigger *"fact hay đoán?"* scales into a
   system rule.
2. Design the mechanism for this brain: candidate = an **epistemic status**
   convention on wiki claims (frontmatter field or inline tags) + a lint check
   for unlabeled strong claims; decide during discussion, then amend CLAUDE.md.
3. Deliverables: `wiki/fact-vs-opinion.md`; schema change in `CLAUDE.md`;
   retrofit pass over existing pages.

## Question 3 — Human society ↔ AI structures

**Thức's starting position:** agents/harnesses/loops (and Hermes, Shepherd)
all mirror human organization — roles, authority, departments; the way product
development produced waterfall → agile (Scrum, Kanban). Working mapping:
agent = team member, harness = the tooling/process around them, loop = sprint.
Goal: discuss deeply enough to see where AI structures should evolve next.

**Agreed plan:**
1. Stress-test the mapping: where the org analogy holds, where it breaks
   (agents copy instantly, don't get tired, don't have careers — see
   [[wiki/agent-org-multiplied-self]]).
2. Ingest the three architecture docs **here**, one at a time (Codez map →
   Hermes → Shepherd), now with the frames from Questions 1–2 to separate
   their facts from their opinions.
3. Deliverables: `wiki/human-org-ai-mapping.md`; concept pages for the three
   docs; a synthesis on "what AI structures still need."

## Working rules for this roadmap

- **Sequential only.** A question opens when the previous one closes.
- **Closing criteria:** Thức can state the concept in his own words, can name
  where it applies to him / the brain / agents, and the deliverable pages are
  filed and linked.
- This page is the status board — the agent updates it as questions progress.
